# Salesforce Partners with Databricks to Help AI Agents Turn Trusted Data into Trusted Action

Artificial Intelligence is only as powerful as the data behind it. While AI agents are becoming increasingly capable of automating tasks, making decisions, and assisting employees, one challenge remains constant: **trustworthy data**.

To address this challenge, Salesforce and Databricks have announced an expanded partnership aimed at helping AI agents access, understand, and act on trusted enterprise data securely and efficiently. The collaboration strengthens the foundation for the next generation of AI-powered business applications and autonomous agents.

## Why This Partnership Matters

Organizations today store massive amounts of data across multiple systems. AI agents often struggle because the data they need is fragmented, duplicated, or governed by different security policies.

Salesforce and Databricks are working to solve this problem by expanding their **Zero Copy architecture**, allowing businesses to access data where it already lives instead of constantly moving or duplicating it. This approach helps reduce complexity while maintaining data integrity and security.

## Key Enhancements

The partnership introduces several new capabilities designed for both human users and AI agents:

* **Federated Authentication** for more seamless and secure access.
* **Identity Mapping** (planned) to maintain consistent user identities across platforms.
* **Governance Interoperability** to align security and compliance controls.
* **Metadata-Aware Access Controls** to ensure agents only access the information they are authorized to use.

These enhancements create a shared trust layer that helps AI agents operate with greater confidence and accuracy.

## A Step Toward Enterprise-Ready AI

As businesses move from AI experimentation to real-world deployment, reliable data access becomes critical. Salesforce's Agentforce ecosystem and Databricks' data intelligence capabilities are being brought closer together to help organizations build AI solutions that can reason over enterprise data and take meaningful actions.

The goal is simple: transform trusted data into trusted action.

## Final Thoughts

The Salesforce–Databricks partnership highlights an important truth about enterprise AI: success isn't just about smarter models—it's about giving those models access to accurate, secure, and governed data.

By strengthening data sharing, security, and governance across both platforms, Salesforce and Databricks are laying the groundwork for AI agents that can deliver real business value while maintaining the trust enterprises require.

As AI adoption continues to accelerate, partnerships like this will play a crucial role in turning AI from a promising technology into a reliable business partner.
